# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Tinlance Agent Platform SDK community is committed to a respectful, constructive, and inclusive environment. We welcome contributions from people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and professionally;
- assume good intent while addressing technical disagreements directly;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and evidence rather than people;
- respect privacy and confidentiality;
- accept constructive feedback;
- keep discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, sexualized conduct, threats, doxxing, and publication of private information without permission are not acceptable.

Security vulnerabilities must not be disclosed through public issues. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact **hello@tinlance.com** with the subject line `Code of Conduct report — Tinlance Agent Platform SDK`.

Reports are reviewed by the project maintainer. The maintainer may remove or edit content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action when necessary.

Please include enough context to investigate the report. Do not include passwords, private keys, access tokens, or unnecessary personal information.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and interactions where participants are representing or materially participating in the Tinlance Agent Platform SDK community.
